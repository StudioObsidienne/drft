# DRFT

**An endless drum & bass radio with a pixel-art racing visualizer, built as a single HTML file.**

DRFT turns your browser into a late-night radio station. Load your own WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 files and they play back-to-back with no gaps and smooth crossfades. The next track is decoded in advance, so there is no wait between songs. Your library is saved in the browser, so it is still there the next time you open the page.

No files? DRFT composes drum & bass in real time across five stations: Retro Racer, Liquid, Jungle, Neurofunk and Atmospheric. Every track is generated on the spot, and any track you like can be saved and replayed exactly.

Behind the music, low-resolution pixel scenes loop forever: a car on a neon highway, drifting through a mountain pass, cruising past a city skyline, lapping a circuit seen from above, or racing through a tunnel. You can also drop in your own car screenshots. Everything reacts to the bass. The weather changes on its own and the colors fade from one mood to the next: rain turns the screen grey, storms flash lightning on the kicks, sunsets glow pink, snow drifts down.

Pause doesn't cut the sound: the music echoes out and dissolves into a tail before going quiet. Buttons answer with soft pixel clicks.

No build step, no dependencies, no server. Open `index.html` or host it on GitHub Pages.
